Sana: service boundaries and cash provenance
Branch: fix/service-boundaries-cash-provenance

Scope
The new calibrated profile is config/event_camera_service.json. It enables
service_boundary and cash_provenance_enabled. The existing event_camera.json
profile and R01-R05 implementations remain unchanged. No weights or ID detector
were replaced. No video names or scenario timestamps are used in decisions.

Root cause established before editing
1. GeometricRoleTracker accepts IoU >= .15 OR centre distance <= .08 and retains
   the matched role. It tracks bodies geometrically, not customer visits.
2. EventDetector keyed presence by that raw ID. A bbox centre inside the broad
   room polygon prevented exit even after the person crossed behind the glass.
3. During short missing intervals people_observable became false, resetting
   absence evidence. The next person inherited the old geometric ID.
4. X-5 reused person-1; X-4 reused person-3. Both previously had one open service.
5. ObjectState was recreated whenever active_session changed, incorrectly
   discarding an already observed cash object's original service.

Boundary implementation
Raw track_id is retained. customer_id identifies a visit; the unchanged engine
allocates service-N session_id. Reused raw IDs receive a new visit only after
positive departure evidence. New raw IDs can recover the same visit using
bounded spatial and temporal continuity. Unexplained jumps remain uncertain.
Departure requires an interior -> portal -> exterior sequence, including three
positive exterior observations spanning at least .4 s. Missing detections alone
never close a service. These are crossing confirmation parameters, not new
absence thresholds. Cashier tracking remains unchanged.

Calibration
Three polygons apply to the upper body anchor (bbox centre x, y1 + .2 * height).
Feet are occluded by the desk and body centres overlap the glass partition.
The geometry is specific to this fixed camera and must be recalibrated if the
camera moves. The interior polygon is conservative: reported service start is
confirmed presence in the service area, later than the actual doorway crossing.
No claim of exact physical entrance time is made.

Cash implementation
Each logical cash observation group stores source_session_id, first_seen,
last_seen, last_known_zone, associated_person_role, counter_interaction_history,
table_presence_history, observation_confidence and provenance_confidence.
Frame histories preserve source_session_id across service changes.
The additional provenance pass preserves original tracking output. It reconnects
fragments only with >=3 observations on both sides, compatible visible size,
spatial/trajectory agreement and a unique predecessor. Existing limits are used:
maximum gap 1.25 s; maximum normalized distance .045; maximum area ratio 2.5.
Concurrent objects are not merged. Sparse, ambiguous or longer gaps are rejected.
Shared TABLE_ZONE alone never establishes identity. Confirmed safe placement
blocks reconnection to a new table object. Session context is only possible
origin until positive transfer evidence confirms it. Recovery runs after a
short incoming observation window; confirmed_at_sec records this delay.
bind_cash_event supplies original operation/customer identity to unchanged R05;
it never upgrades uncertain observations.
